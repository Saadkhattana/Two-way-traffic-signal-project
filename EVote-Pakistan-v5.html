About EVote Pakistan

Pakistan's Secure
Digital Voting Platform

EVote Pakistan is a modern, tamper-proof electronic voting system designed to bring transparency, accessibility, and trust to Pakistan's democratic process. Built by students of Air university islamabad, it demonstrates how technology can strengthen civic participation.

End-to-End Security

Every vote is protected through multi-layer security: CNIC-based identity verification, strong password enforcement, session management, and automatic inactivity timeouts.

Full Transparency

Real-time results are visible to all registered voters. Admins have access to live analytics, turnout data, and a comprehensive activity log that records every system event.

Accessible by Design

A clean, minimal interface works on any device and browser. Citizens register once with their CNIC and vote from anywhere — no special hardware required.

Admin Oversight

A dedicated admin panel lets authorised personnel manage candidates, control election start/stop, monitor voter registrations, and export full audit trails.

One Person, One Vote

The system strictly enforces the fundamental voting principle. Each CNIC can only cast a single ballot, and the voter's record is immediately flagged after submission.

Live Analytics

Admins monitor voter turnout, participation percentages, leading candidates, and vote distribution in real time — empowering data-driven election oversight.

Our Mission

We believe every Pakistani citizen deserves a secure, convenient way to exercise their democratic right. EVote Pakistan is a proof-of-concept that demonstrates how modern web technology — when built responsibly — can modernise electoral processes, reduce fraud, and increase voter engagement across the country.

How It Works

Simple. Secure. Step by Step.

From registration to result — here's exactly how the EVote Pakistan platform works for voters and administrators.

1

Voter Registration

Any Pakistani citizen aged 18 or above can register using their CNIC number, full name, and date of birth. The system automatically validates the CNIC format and verifies that the applicant meets the minimum age requirement. A secure password is created during registration.

2

Admin Approval

Newly registered voters are assigned a "Pending" status. The election administrator reviews and approves voter accounts through the Admin Panel. Only approved voters can log in and cast a ballot. Suspicious accounts can be banned at any time.

3

Secure Voter Login

Approved voters log in with their CNIC and password. Multiple failed login attempts trigger a temporary lockout to prevent brute-force attacks. Sessions are protected with automatic 15-minute inactivity timeouts to safeguard unattended devices.

4

Casting a Vote

Once logged in, the voter sees the list of registered candidates along with their party and symbol. The voter selects one candidate and clicks "Cast Vote." A confirmation dialogue shows the selected candidate before the vote is finalised — giving the voter a chance to review their choice.

5

Vote Recorded & Locked

After submission, the vote is stored and the voter's account is marked as "Voted." The voter can still view live results but cannot cast another ballot. Every action — login, vote, admin change — is recorded in the system's immutable activity log.

6

Live Results & Export

Results update in real time as votes come in. Both the admin and the voter dashboards display vote counts, percentages, and rankings. Admins can export full results as CSV, download the voter registry, and access the complete audit log for post-election verification.
